PHILIPPINES · MENTAL HEALTH & CRISIS SUPPORT

NCMH Crisis Hotline

Free phone support in the Philippines for emotional distress, mental health crises, and suicide prevention.

24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

1800-1888-1553

Service information

You could start with: “I’ve been having a difficult time, and I’d like someone to talk to.” You can choose how much to say.

You might ask a trusted person to sit with you or help you contact a professional. If a service is unavailable, try another trusted person, qualified professional, or local emergency service.

Directory information checked 17 October 2026. Availability may change. This external service is not operated by Hinga. Hinga is not a clinical service or a staffed crisis channel.
